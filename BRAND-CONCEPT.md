# KICKZ.LK Premium Website Concept

## 1. Creative Direction

**Positioning:** A premium, trusted gateway to global sneaker culture for Sri Lanka.

**Core idea:** **“The Drop Room”** — the website feels like entering a members-only digital showroom rather than browsing a standard online shop.

**Visual language:**
- Near-black luxury foundation with white editorial typography.
- Neon acid green used only for action, verification and drop energy.
- Oversized type, strong negative space and magazine-style product composition.
- Glass panels, fine borders, controlled glow and subtle texture.
- Product imagery floats inside dark gallery spaces to create a collectible-object feeling.

**Brand personality:** Exclusive, direct, confident, culturally aware and trustworthy.

## 3. Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Main background | Obsidian black | `#070707` |
| Secondary background | Carbon | `#0B0B0D` |
| Elevated surface | Graphite | `#151518` |
| Primary typography | Bone white | `#F5F5EF` |
| Secondary typography | Concrete grey | `#A7A7A2` |
| Primary accent | Acid green | `#B8FF35` |
| Luxury accent | Champagne gold | `#D4B276` |
| Lifestyle accent | Mint | `#78F7D0` |

The acid green is intentionally limited to CTAs, live indicators, authentication and selected states. This keeps the brand premium instead of gaming-oriented.

## 4. Typography

- **Display:** Syne 700–800 — bold, wide and editorial for headlines.
- **Interface/body:** Space Grotesk 400–700 — modern, clean and highly readable on mobile.
- **Fallbacks:** Arial Black, Helvetica Neue and Arial.

Typography hierarchy:
- Hero headline: 58–110 px desktop, 49–76 px mobile
- Section headline: 42–76 px desktop
- Product title: 43–68 px
- Body copy: 13–17 px
- Utility labels: 9–11 px with increased letter spacing

## 5. Component System

- Sticky transparent header with blurred background
- Acid-green pill CTA and dark outline CTA
- Product cards with cinematic stage, badges, save button and SKU
- Glass trust labels and floating pricing labels
- Brand tiles with oversized wordmarks
- Numbered process steps
- Verified review cards
- Interactive size chips and payment cards
- WhatsApp conversion button
- Mobile bottom purchase bar
- Scroll-reveal animation system
- Product image parallax and hover focus

## 6. Mobile UX Strategy

- One-column product catalogue below 650 px
- Large tap targets and horizontal filter scrolling
- Simplified mobile menu with oversized editorial links
- Gallery thumbnails move below the main product image
- Four-column size selector
- Fixed mobile WhatsApp and pre-order actions
- Reduced decorative content near key conversion areas

## 7. UI Inspiration Translation

- **GOAT / StockX:** Trust, verification and market clarity
- **KITH / END:** Editorial layouts and high-end brand presentation
- **Nike SNKRS:** Drop energy, bold storytelling and scarcity cues
- **A Ma Maniére:** Luxury spacing, restraint and cultural tone

The final direction does not directly copy any reference interface. It combines these principles into a distinctive KICKZ.LK system built around the supplied logo.

## 8. Production Photography Direction

The included local SVG sneaker visuals keep the demo lightweight and fully offline. For production, replace them with:
- Transparent PNG/WebP side-profile product cut-outs
- Minimum 1800 px wide
- Soft directional studio light
- 30–45 degree hero angle
- Genuine outsole and material details visible
- Consistent shadow direction across the catalogue
- Editorial lifestyle photographs cropped 4:5 and 16:9

## 9. Conversion and Trust Notes

Before launch, replace the placeholder WhatsApp number, social URLs, policies and product inventory. Add a verified payment gateway, CMS/product database, order tracking, analytics, product schema, image optimization and a formal authenticity/pre-order policy.
